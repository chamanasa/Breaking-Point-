Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everything here is free. The AI interviewer runs on your own free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio, so there is no subscription and nothing to pay.

How does the AI interviewer work?

It plays the interviewer for the question you pick: it asks you to clarify and structure, pushes back on your assumptions and scores you on six criteria when you finish. Your answers go straight from your browser to Google.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use everything as a guest, but nothing is saved. Sign in with Google to keep your progress, drill levels and firm plans on any device.

Can I practise out loud?

Yes. Tap the waveform in an interview and talk: the interviewer listens, answers aloud and keeps the conversation going until you finish. Voice works best in Chrome.

Which firms are covered?

Fifteen, from McKinsey, BCG and Bain to Kearney, Redseer and Indus Insights. Each has its interview format, what it screens for, a plan built around your interview date and mocks in its style.

What about market entry, profitability and other cases?

They are on the way: market entry, profitability, pricing, growth, M&A and due diligence, and unconventional cases. Guesstimates, maths drills, firm prep and progress tracking are live today.
